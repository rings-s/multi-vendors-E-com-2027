# Product Overview

## Product concept

Build a Saudi multi-vendor commerce platform connecting customers with independent merchants.

The platform operates as an intermediary marketplace.

Each merchant has an independent store, products/services, pricing, orders, customer relationships, financial records, and settlement information.

The marketplace operator earns revenue through a commission on successful transactions.

### Core business model

Example:

- Customer purchases: 100 SAR.
- Platform commission: 10%.
- Platform commission: 10 SAR.
- Merchant proceeds: 90 SAR.

The payment provider processes the payment and distributes the funds according to the approved marketplace settlement arrangement.

The platform must never depend on manually collecting all customer payments into its own bank account and transferring seller earnings afterward.

### Geographic scope

- Initial market: Saudi Arabia.
- Currency: SAR.
- Language: Arabic and English.
- Arabic must support proper RTL layouts.
- English must support LTR layouts.
- Phone numbers must support Saudi numbers (+966).
- Addresses must support Saudi administrative regions and cities.
- Merchant bank accounts must support Saudi IBANs.

### Product scope

Build a flexible marketplace supporting:

1. Independent stores.
2. Physical products.
3. Services.
4. Product and service categories.
5. Merchant storefronts.
6. Customer shopping carts.
7. Checkout and payment.
8. Order management.
9. Automatic commission calculation.
10. Merchant financial dashboards.
11. Automatic payment splitting.
12. Direct merchant settlement.
13. Marketplace administration.

Design the catalog and order model so that physical products and services can have different fulfillment workflows.
